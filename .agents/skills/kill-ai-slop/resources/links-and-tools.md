## The Five Telltale Signs of AI Slop

Write these somewhere visible. If your output has any of these, it's slop:

1. **Typography** — wrong scale, bad weight choices, too many typefaces
2. **Imagery** — stock photo feel, mismatched style, no focal point
3. **Hierarchy** — nothing leads the eye, everything is the same visual weight
4. **Color** — too many colors, no dominant/accent structure, bad contrast
5. **Spacing** — inconsistent padding, elements too tight or too loose
