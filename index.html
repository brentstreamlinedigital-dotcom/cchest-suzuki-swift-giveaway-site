The Journey to the Suzuki Swift

Here is exactly how the raffle operates, from buying your ticket to the final live-draw event.

Step 1

Get Registered

Purchase a ticket package securely on this website. You'll receive your electronic entry numbers instantly via email confirmation.

Step 2

Monthly Finalists Drawn

Once a month, for 8 months, one winner is drawn using a secure computer-based random engine. These 8 finalists are guaranteed a prize and are invited to the final event.

Step 3

The Grand Key Event

At the 75th Anniversary Gala, all 8 finalists assemble on stage. Eight hats are placed on a table, each containing a car key. Only one key starts the brand new Suzuki Swift.

Step 4

Starting the Swift

Each finalist selects a hat in turn and tries their key in the ignition. The finalist whose key successfully starts the engine wins and drives home in the Suzuki Swift!
